# Related Projects

PPoS Dex measures how the online stake is shared. These projects look at
Algorand decentralization from other angles, and help read its numbers.

## Nodely

[Nodely](https://g.nodely.io/d/network/network) observes the network from its
relays: how many nodes run, where they are hosted and which version they run,
along with the consensus and the protocol upgrades. It counts the nodes with
the [Chao-1 estimator](../project/nodes.md#chao-1-estimator), and shows the
[infrastructure diversity](../project/topology.md#infrastructure-diversity)
the stake alone does not.

## Valar Decentralization

[Valar Decentralization](https://decentralization.valar.solutions/) shows who
is behind the online stake: the stake by operator, staking solution, account
type and owner, with the coefficients of named entities. Where PPoS Dex groups
the accounts into [likely operators](../project/stake.md#nakamoto-coefficient)
by heuristic, its labels catch the custodians and staking providers running
many accounts.

## Partlife

[Partlife](https://partlife.app/) follows the participation keys of the online
accounts: when they expire, and which accounts are eligible for payouts,
absent or suspended. Keys expiring together are a risk to
[liveness](../project/stake.md#nakamoto-coefficient) that the stake alone does
not show.

[Ⱥ Surf Upgrades](https://algo.surf/upgrades) lists every consensus upgrade,

[Upgrade Coefficient](../project/stake.md#nakamoto-coefficient) in practice.

The Algorand Pure Proof of Stake Decentralization Index (by
[cusma](https://cusma.algo.xyz)): how decentralized the stake securing Algorand
is, measured from the ledger and published on-chain every day.

## Start Here

- **The index**: what [PPoS Dex](project/what-is-ppos-dex.md) is, why it
  [measures decentralization](project/decentralization.md) the way it does, and
  its [definition](project/ppos-dex.md).
- **The data**: the published history as [timeseries](data/timeseries.md), the
  latest data point as a [snapshot](data/snapshot.md), and the
  [related projects](data/related-projects.md) that look at Algorand from other
  angles.
- **The CLI**: [install](cli/install.md) it to publish data points, plot them,
  or export them for your own analysis.
